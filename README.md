# PaisaMap

Wrapped by PaisaMap: see where your money goes, every month and the whole year, set monthly limits and get warned before you overspend.

- Reads PhonePe and bank statements (PDF, Excel, CSV) in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.
- Saves payments, labels and limits in the browser on that phone only (localStorage). "Delete my data" wipes them.
- Installable (PWA). On Android, share a bank SMS to the app to add a payment.

Live at https://paisamap.online
