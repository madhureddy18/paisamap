# PaisaMap

PaisaMap: see where your money goes, every month and the whole year, set monthly limits and get warned before you overspend.

- Reads PhonePe and bank statements (PDF, Excel, CSV) in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.
- Statements and payments are never saved. Only your limits and shop labels are kept in the browser on that phone; "Reset" wipes them.
- Installable (PWA). On Android, share a bank SMS to the app to add a payment.

Live at https://paisamap.online
