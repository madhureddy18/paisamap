Privacy

Short version: your statement never leaves your phone.

Your statement

When you pick a statement, it's opened and read by this web page inside your own browser. It is not uploaded to any server, not sent to us, and not saved. When you close the page, it's gone.

What stays on your phone

Only your monthly limits and the shop labels you choose (like "Ramesh K → Tiffin"), saved in your browser so you don't have to set them again. "Reset my limits & labels" deletes them.

Visit counting

We count page visits and a few anonymous events (like "statement read" or "card shared") with GoatCounter. No cookies, no names, no amounts, nothing from your statement.

Reviews

If you rate PaisaMap, we get only what you choose in the review box: your stars, what you type, and (if you tap it) which app or bank your statement was from. Nothing from your statement is attached. Reviews are stored in a private Google Form that only we can read.

If a statement doesn't work

You can copy a short note (bank name, file type, size) and send it to us on Instagram. Never send the statement itself.

Not financial advice

Categories are a best guess from payment names. Suggestions are general, not financial advice.
