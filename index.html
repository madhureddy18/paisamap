PaisaMap 🔒 stays on your phone

Where did your money go?

Upload your bank or PhonePe statement. PaisaMap shows every month, the whole year, the small spends that add up, and how much you can save.

Your statement is read on this phone. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

What you get

  • 📈Every monthhow much went out, and the costliest month
  • 🪙Small spendsyour ₹20–₹100 payments, added up
  • 🔁Repeatssubscriptions and bills that come back every month
  • 🚦Monthly limitsa warning at 80%, and when you cross it
  • 💰Your planwhat to cut, and what you save in a year

Getting your statement

PhonePe

PhonePe → History → Download statement (top right) → pick the months. It arrives by email as a PDF; the password is usually in that email.

Your bank (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis…)

Net banking or the bank app → Accounts → Statement → choose dates → download as Excel/CSV (best) or PDF.

Google Pay or Paytm

These pay from your bank account, so your bank statement already has every payment.
