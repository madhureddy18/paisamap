How to get your statement

PhonePe

Open PhonePe → History → tap Download statement (top right) → pick the months → it comes to your email as a PDF. The PDF password is usually in that email.

Your bank (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis…)

Net banking or the bank app → Accounts → Statement → choose the dates → download as Excel/CSV (best) or PDF.

Google Pay / Paytm

UPI apps pay from your bank account, so your bank statement already has these payments. Use that.
